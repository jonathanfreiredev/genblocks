# What this registry does not publish

These are not style opinions. They are the shapes a model produces when it is
asked for a page and stops thinking, and they are what a judge reads as "nobody
decided anything here". They cost in `visual` and in `copy`.

- **The centred column of everything.** Headline, subhead, two buttons, all
  centred, all the same width, nothing weighing more than anything else.
- **Four equal cards.** Same size, same weight, an icon at the top, a two-word
  title, a sentence of filler under it. A row of cards is a rhythm; four identical
  ones are a shrug.
- **The dark hero with a gradient glow.** A look, and a tell when there is no idea
  underneath it. If it is dark, something in it has to be doing the work that the
  darkness is hiding.
- **Copy with nothing in it**: «Acme Inc.», «Lorem ipsum», «Your tagline here»,
  «Click here», a feature called «Fast» with the sentence «It is fast». Write what
  a real product of this kind would say, and put a real noun in it.
- **Decorative gradients as hierarchy**: making something important by making it
  loud, and then louder when that did not work.
- **An icon per bullet repeating the bullet's own words.**
- **Everything a `div`**: clickable boxes, fake buttons, bold text where a heading
  belongs. `accessibility` charges for each one of them.
- **Five sections that are the same section.** The brief asked for a page; the
  registry publishes a block, and a block that repeats itself has one idea.
- **Placeholder geometry**: a 16:9 box with nothing in it, an avatar circle with
  initials in it, a chart drawn as three bars of arbitrary heights. If it stands in
  for content, it has to look like it was measured.
