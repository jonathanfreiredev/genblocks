Images can be generated for a block or requested on their own. Every image is public stock once it passes the gates. Pictures made for a block and pictures made independently live in the same library.

You may describe the picture you want. That direction is checked before the task is queued. A picture with people is refused. If the person is incidental, the product may offer a no-person rewrite and proceeds only if you accept it. If the person is the subject, the request is refused. If a component brief says nothing about images, the builder chooses the pictures.

## 9. Moderation, reports, and enforcement

### 9.1 Moderation

Text is moderated before a task is queued and again after the block is written. Standalone image prompts pass a policy preflight, and the prompt is checked again before generation. Image bytes and rendered screenshots are checked by the no-people detector before they can reach a public asset host, and then by the generic safety classifier.

Production fails closed when moderation or the no-people detector is not configured. A missing provider is an outage, not permission to publish. Every moderation decision is recorded with the reason and the policy version.

Generated artifacts are published after automated moderation and can be removed by an operator. The operator receives one notification per generated block and per generated image, after publication. The notification is not an approval gate. A generated image containing a person is refused, not edited silently. The operator receives a metadata-only notice with the brief, the user, the reason, and an image hash. The image itself is not attached or stored with that notice.
